Core Features 核心功能特點

Real-time Data Visualization 實時數據可視化

Transform abstract EEG signals into intuitive charts. Visualize your concentration fluctuations in real-time, just as clearly as a heartbeat, helping you adjust your learning state more intelligently. 將抽象的腦電波（EEG）轉化為簡單易懂的圖表。你能即時看到自己的專注力起伏，就像看到心跳一樣直觀，幫助你更聰明地調整學習狀態。

3D Immersive Engine 3D 沉浸式引擎

An interactive environment built with WebGL technology. No keyboard or mouse required; your "focus" is the controller. As you concentrate, the game environment evolves positively, achieving true mind-interaction. 使用 WebGL 技術打造的互動環境。不需要鍵盤，你的「專注」就是控制器。當你越專心，遊戲環境就會產生正向變化，實現真正的意念互動。

Device Connectivity 裝置連接

Supports mainstream EEG headsets like Muse and Emotiv via Bluetooth 5.0 high-speed connection. Plug-and-play ensures every thought is instantly captured and fed back by the system. 支援 Muse、Emotiv 等主流腦電波儀器，透過藍牙連接。確保你的每一個念頭都能被系統即時捕捉並反饋。

Transforming Abstract Signals into Interactive Experiences 將抽象信號
轉化為互動體驗

The NeuroFocus system precisely analyzes your brainwave data, extracting Alpha waves representing relaxation and Beta waves representing focus. We bridge the gap between your brain activity and the digital world, making "cognitive training" tangible data rather than abstract theory. NeuroFocus 系統能精確分析你的腦波數據，重點提取代表放鬆的 Alpha 波與代表專注的 Beta 波。我們在你的大腦活動與數位世界之間架起橋樑，讓「認知訓練」不再是空泛的理論，而是看得見的數據。
