## 材料：細胞膜與物質運輸（基礎）　/　Cell Membrane & Transport (Foundation)　（基礎 Foundation · 初中）

### 第 1 節 · 這一課學什麼　/　Section 1 · What This Lesson Covers

*這份筆記講「細胞膜與物質運輸」——細胞怎樣控制物質進出。讀完你會明白差異透性、擴散、滲透與主動運輸四個概念。*

*These notes cover the cell membrane and transport — how cells control what moves in and out. You will understand four ideas: differential permeability, diffusion, osmosis, and active transport.*

#### 本課大綱　/　Lesson Outline

- 細胞膜的差異透性
  - Differential permeability of the membrane
- 擴散
  - Diffusion
- 滲透
  - Osmosis
- 主動運輸
  - Active transport

> **【重點 / Key】** 重點：細胞膜是「守門員」，控制哪些物質可以進出細胞。
>
> Key idea: the cell membrane is a “gatekeeper” controlling what may enter or leave the cell.

### 第 2 節 · 細胞膜的差異透性　/　Section 2 · Differential Permeability

所有活細胞外都有細胞膜，負責控制物質進出。細胞膜不會隨便讓所有物質穿過，只容許特定分子通過，這個特性稱為差異透性。

Every living cell is surrounded by a membrane that controls what moves in and out. It does not let everything through — only certain molecules pass. This property is called differential (selective) permeability.

細胞需要持續交換物質維持生命：氧氣、水分、養分進入細胞；二氧化碳、代謝廢物排出，全部依賴細胞膜運輸。

Cells must keep exchanging materials to stay alive: oxygen, water and nutrients enter; carbon dioxide and waste leave — all across the membrane.

> **【重點 / Key】** 一旦細胞膜失去控制，細胞會快速死亡。
>
> If the membrane loses this control, the cell dies quickly.

### 第 3 節 · 擴散　/　Section 3 · Diffusion

分子會自發由高濃度區域移動到低濃度區域，這個過程叫擴散，不需要消耗細胞能量。

Molecules spontaneously move from an area of higher concentration to one of lower concentration. This is diffusion, and it needs no energy from the cell.

- **例子 / Example**：肺部吸入的氧氣透過擴散進入體細胞；細胞產生的二氧化碳同樣靠擴散離開細胞。
  - Oxygen taken in by the lungs diffuses into body cells; the carbon dioxide the cells make diffuses out the same way.

> **【重點 / Key】** 當細胞內外濃度相同時，分子的淨移動會停止。
>
> When the concentration is equal inside and outside, the net movement of molecules stops.

### 第 4 節 · 滲透　/　Section 4 · Osmosis

滲透是水分專用的擴散：水分透過選擇性通透膜，由水多（溶質少）的一邊移向水少（溶質多）的一邊。

Osmosis is diffusion of water only: water moves across a selectively permeable membrane from where there is more water (less solute) to where there is less water (more solute).

- 植物細胞放入清水：水分流入，細胞膨脹，植物挺直。
  - Plant cell in pure water: water flows in, the cell swells, the plant stays firm and upright.
- 植物細胞放入濃鹽水：水分流出，細胞軟縮，植物會凋萎。
  - Plant cell in strong salt water: water flows out, the cell shrinks, the plant wilts.

> **【重點 / Key】** 滲透只描述「水分」的移動。
>
> Osmosis describes the movement of water only.

### 第 5 節 · 主動運輸　/　Section 5 · Active Transport

有時細胞需要把物質由低濃度運去高濃度，和自然擴散方向相反，這就是主動運輸。主動運輸必須消耗細胞儲存的能量。

Sometimes a cell must move substances from low to high concentration — the opposite of natural diffusion. This is active transport, and it must use the cell’s stored energy.

- **例子 / Example**：小腸細胞吸收葡萄糖、植物根部吸收礦物離子。
  - Small-intestine cells absorbing glucose; plant roots absorbing mineral ions.

> **【重點 / Key】** 總結：差異透性負責守門；擴散和滲透不需能量；主動運輸逆濃度、需要能量。
>
> Summary: differential permeability guards the gate; diffusion and osmosis need no energy; active transport goes against the gradient and needs energy.

### 第 2 節 · 細胞膜流動鑲嵌模型　/　Section 2 · The Fluid-Mosaic Model

細胞膜的骨架是磷脂雙層：磷脂頭親水，分別朝向細胞內外的水溶液；磷脂尾疏水，藏在雙層中央，阻隔水溶性大分子直接穿膜。

The membrane’s framework is a phospholipid bilayer: the water-loving heads face the watery solutions inside and outside, while the water-fearing tails hide in the centre, blocking water-soluble large molecules from crossing directly.

膜內鑲嵌多種蛋白：通道蛋白、載體蛋白、糖蛋白。磷脂層可流動、蛋白能在膜內橫向移動，因此稱為「流動鑲嵌模型」，解釋了細胞膜差異透性的根本原因。

Embedded in the membrane are various proteins: channel proteins, carrier proteins and glycoproteins. The phospholipids flow and the proteins drift sideways within the membrane — hence the “fluid-mosaic model”, which explains the root cause of differential permeability.

- **通道／載體蛋白 / Channel / carrier proteins**：協助特定分子（如帶電或大分子）跨膜運輸的通道或搬運工。
  - Passages or shuttles that help specific molecules (e.g. charged or large ones) cross the membrane.

### 第 3 節 · 滲透對動、植物細胞的差異　/　Section 3 · Osmosis: Animal vs Plant Cells

在同樣濃度的溶液下，動物與植物細胞的表現完全不同。

In the same solution, animal and plant cells behave very differently.

- **動物細胞 / Animal cells**：沒有細胞壁：低濃溶液吸水會膨脹甚至破裂，高濃溶液失水軟縮。因此人體注射要用等濃生理鹽水。
  - No cell wall: in a dilute solution they swell and may burst; in a concentrated one they shrink. That is why injections use isotonic (equal-concentration) saline.

- **植物細胞 / Plant cells**：外有細胞壁支撐：吸水只會變硬挺、不會破裂；長期置於高濃溶液會失水，產生「質壁分離」現象。
  - A cell wall supports them: taking in water makes them firm without bursting; left in a concentrated solution they lose water, causing plasmolysis (the membrane pulling away from the wall).

> **【重點 / Key】** 質壁分離只會在有細胞壁的植物細胞出現。
>
> Plasmolysis only occurs in plant cells, which have a cell wall.

### 第 4 節 · 氣體交換中的擴散應用　/　Section 4 · Diffusion in Gas Exchange

人體肺部、植物葉片氣孔都依靠擴散交換氣體。肺泡壁細胞很薄，空氣中氧氣濃度高，自動擴散入血液；細胞產生的二氧化碳濃度高，反向擴散排出體外。

Both the lungs and the stomata of plant leaves rely on diffusion to exchange gases. Alveolar walls are very thin; oxygen is more concentrated in the air, so it diffuses into the blood, while carbon dioxide (more concentrated in cells) diffuses out.

葉片進行光合作用時，二氧化碳經氣孔擴散進葉肉細胞，產生的氧氣再擴散離開。

During photosynthesis, carbon dioxide diffuses through the stomata into the leaf cells, and the oxygen produced diffuses back out.

> **【重點 / Key】** 薄的交換表面 + 濃度差 = 高效擴散。
>
> A thin exchange surface plus a concentration difference means efficient diffusion.

### 第 5 節 · 植物如何透過滲透支撐軀幹　/　Section 5 · Osmosis and Plant Support

草本植物沒有木質硬莖，依靠細胞吸水產生的膨脹來支撐枝葉。

Herbaceous (non-woody) plants have no hard woody stem; they rely on the swelling of water-filled cells to hold up their stems and leaves.

當根吸收水分，水分透過滲透進入莖、葉細胞；細胞膨脹後對細胞壁產生壓力（膨脹壓），令植物挺直。長期缺水時細胞失水、膨脹消失，花草就會凋謝枯萎。

When roots absorb water, it passes by osmosis into stem and leaf cells. The swollen cells press on their walls (turgor pressure), keeping the plant upright. In a long drought the cells lose water, the turgor is lost, and the plant wilts.

- **膨脹壓（膨壓） / Turgor pressure**：細胞吸水膨脹後對細胞壁施加的壓力，是草本植物挺直的來源。
  - The pressure a swollen cell exerts on its wall — the source of firmness in soft-stemmed plants.

### 第 6 節 · 延伸：運輸方式的能量與比較　/　Section 6 · Extension: Energy & a Transport Comparison

*（進階延伸）把四種運輸方式放在一起比較，關鍵問題只有一個：需不需要能量？*

*(Advanced extension) Comparing the transport types side by side, the key question is just one: is energy needed?*

- **被動運輸 / Passive transport**：擴散與滲透都沿濃度梯度進行、不需消耗細胞能量（ATP）。
  - Diffusion and osmosis both follow the concentration gradient and use no cell energy (ATP).

- **主動運輸 / Active transport**：逆濃度梯度、需要載體蛋白並消耗 ATP，例如根部由稀薄土壤吸收礦物離子。
  - Goes against the gradient, needs carrier proteins and uses ATP — e.g. roots absorbing mineral ions from dilute soil.

為什麼細胞普遍細小？因為物質靠表面進出、卻要供應整個體積；細胞越小，表面積對體積的比例越大，交換越有效率。

Why are cells generally small? Substances enter across the surface but must supply the whole volume; the smaller the cell, the larger its surface-area-to-volume ratio and the more efficient the exchange.

> **【重點 / Key】** 一句記法：被動運輸「順流免費」，主動運輸「逆流收費（ATP）」。
>
> One-line memory: passive transport is “downhill and free”, active transport is “uphill and paid for (ATP)”.

- **D.** 促進主動運輸　/　To boost active transport

> **答案 / Answer：B** — 等濃鹽水令水分進出平衡，血細胞不會膨脹破裂或軟縮。　/　Isotonic saline balances water movement so blood cells neither burst nor shrink.

**6.** 質壁分離現象只會在哪類細胞出現？

　　Plasmolysis occurs only in which kind of cell?

- **A.** 動物細胞　/　Animal cells
- **B.** 植物細胞　/　Plant cells
- **C.** 血液紅細胞　/　Red blood cells
- **D.** 肺泡細胞　/　Alveolar cells

> **答案 / Answer：B** — 質壁分離是細胞膜脫離細胞壁，只在有細胞壁的植物細胞發生。　/　Plasmolysis is the membrane pulling from the wall — only in plant cells, which have a wall.

**7.** 植物葉片吸收二氧化碳進行光合作用依靠什麼機制？

　　How do leaves take in carbon dioxide for photosynthesis?

- **A.** 主動運輸　/　Active transport
- **B.** 滲透　/　Osmosis
- **C.** 擴散　/　Diffusion
- **D.** 膨脹　/　Swelling

> **答案 / Answer：C** — 二氧化碳經氣孔擴散進葉肉細胞。　/　Carbon dioxide diffuses through the stomata into the leaf cells.

**8.** 草本植物枝葉可以挺直，依靠細胞產生的？

　　Soft-stemmed plants stay upright because their cells generate?

- **A.** 膨脹壓　/　Turgor pressure
- **B.** 滲透壓　/　Osmotic pressure
- **C.** 濃度差　/　A concentration difference
- **D.** 化學能量　/　Chemical energy

> **答案 / Answer：A** — 細胞吸水膨脹，對細胞壁產生膨脹壓，支撐植物挺直。　/　Water-filled cells press on their walls (turgor pressure), holding the plant up.

**9.** 下列哪一組全部都不需要消耗細胞能量（ATP）？

　　Which pair are BOTH processes that use no cell energy (ATP)?

- **A.** 擴散與滲透　/　Diffusion and osmosis
- **B.** 主動運輸與滲透　/　Active transport and osmosis
- **C.** 主動運輸與擴散　/　Active transport and diffusion
- **D.** 主動運輸與質壁分離　/　Active transport and plasmolysis

> **答案 / Answer：A** — 擴散與滲透都是被動運輸，沿濃度梯度、不需 ATP。　/　Diffusion and osmosis are both passive — down the gradient, no ATP needed.

**10.** 為什麼細胞通常都很細小？

　　Why are cells usually very small?

- **A.** 細胞越小，表面積對體積比越大，物質交換越有效率　/　Smaller cells have a larger surface-area-to-volume ratio for efficient exchange
- **B.** 細小才能容納細胞核　/　Only small cells can hold a nucleus
- **C.** 大細胞不能分裂　/　Large cells cannot divide
- **D.** 細小的細胞不需要能量　/　Small cells need no energy

> **答案 / Answer：A** — 物質靠表面進出卻要供應整個體積，細胞越小交換越有效率。　/　Substances enter across the surface but supply the whole volume, so smaller cells exchange more efficiently.
